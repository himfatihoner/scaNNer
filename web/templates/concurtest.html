Probe a target's practical concurrency limit using a ramp test, sustained-load test, and burst test.

Probe Mode

Ramp Test (always run)

Steps through concurrency levels 1, 2, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100, 200, … up to the cap. At each level it fires N requests in parallel and reports success rate, p50/p95/p99 latency, status-code mix and error breakdown. The "practical max" is the highest level that holds ≥98% success and p95 within 2× baseline.

Up to 10 000 (C10K). Past that needs OS-level tuning.

Request Shape

Use for POST/PUT/PATCH endpoints (search APIs, GraphQL, login).
